About Community Skills Hub

Who We Are

Community Skills Hub was created to support adults in developing the digital and employability skills needed in modern life.

We provide welcoming and accessible training designed to help people build confidence, improve opportunities, and participate more fully in their communities.

Our Mission

To make digital learning accessible, practical, and supportive for everyone in the community.

Our Vision

To create a future where everyone has the confidence and skills to thrive in a digital world.

Our Values

Inclusion, accessibility, patience, confidence-building, and lifelong learning.
