About Community Skills Hub

Who We Are

Community Skills Hub is a community-focused learning organisation dedicated to helping adults feel confident in an increasingly digital world. We understand that technology can sometimes feel overwhelming, especially for people returning to learning after many years or facing barriers to education and employment.

Our goal is to create a supportive environment where learners feel comfortable asking questions, building practical skills, and progressing at their own pace. Whether someone wants to improve their career prospects, connect with others online, manage everyday digital tasks, or simply grow in confidence, we aim to make learning approachable and meaningful.

We believe education should be accessible, inclusive, and empowering. Through patient support, hands-on workshops, and real-world learning opportunities, Community Skills Hub helps people take positive steps toward greater independence, confidence, and opportunity.

Our Mission

To make digital learning accessible, practical, and supportive for everyone in the community.

Our Vision

To create a future where everyone has the confidence and skills to thrive in a digital world.

Our Values

Inclusion, accessibility, patience, confidence-building, and lifelong learning.

Who We Help

Older Adults

We support older adults in gaining digital confidence to stay connected with loved ones, access services, and enjoy hobbies online.

Job Seekers

Our courses help job seekers build the skills needed to create CVs, apply for jobs online, and prepare for interviews.

Low Income Individuals

We provide free and low-cost training to help those on a tight budget gain valuable skills without financial barriers.
