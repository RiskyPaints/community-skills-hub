Welcome to Community Skills Hub

Empowering Adults Through Digital Skills

At Community Skills Hub, we believe everyone should have access to the skills needed to succeed in modern life. Whether you're learning how to use a computer for the first time, improving your CV, or building confidence online, our friendly and supportive courses are designed to help you progress at your own pace. Our training is practical, accessible, and focused on real-world skills that can make a difference in everyday life and employment opportunities.

Popular Courses

Explore some of our most popular learning pathways designed to help adults gain confidence, improve job prospects, and stay safe online.

Digital Skills

Learn how to stay safe online and avoid scams.
