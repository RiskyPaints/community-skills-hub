Welcome to Community Skills Hub

Our Courses

Build Skills. Build Confidence. Build Your Future.

Community Skills Hub course are designed to help adults build confidence and practical skills in an increasingly digital world. We understand that technology can sometimes feel overwhelming, especially for people returning to learning after many years or facing barriers to education and employment.

Our courses cover a range of topics, from basic digital literacy and online communication to job search skills, financial management, and more. We focus on creating a supportive environment where learners feel comfortable asking questions, building practical skills, and progressing at their own pace.

What You Can Expect

  • Friendly and supportive tutors
  • Practical, easy-to-follow learning
  • Small group sessions and personalised support
  • Courses suitable for beginners and improvers
  • A welcoming community environment

Our Course Offerings

We offer a variety of courses to meet the needs of our learners. Whether you're looking to improve your digital skills, enhance your employability, or simply learn something new, we have something for you.

  • Digital Skills: Learn how to confidently use computers, email, online services, and essential software for work and daily life.
  • Employability & Career Support: Develop workplace skills, improve your CV, prepare for interviews, and build confidence for employment opportunities.
  • Job Search Skills: Get support with writing CVs, preparing for interviews, and finding job opportunities.
  • Financial Management: Learn how to manage your money online, including budgeting and using online banking.
  • Creative Skills: Explore courses in photography, graphic design, and more to express yourself creatively.

Learning that works for you

Everyone learns differently. Our aim is to create an environment where learners feel comfortable asking questions, trying new things, and progressing at their own pace. No pressure, no judgement — just practical support and opportunities to grow.

Ready to Get Started?

If you're interested in joining our courses or want to learn more, please don't hesitate to contact us. We're here to help you take the first step towards building your skills and confidence.
