Welcome to Community Skills Hub

Our Courses

Build Skills. Build Confidence. Build Your Future.

Community Skills Hub course are designed to help adults build confidence and practical skills in an increasingly digital world. We understand that technology can sometimes feel overwhelming, especially for people returning to learning after many years or facing barriers to education and employment.

Our courses cover a range of topics, from basic digital literacy and online communication to job search skills, financial management, and more. We focus on creating a supportive environment where learners feel comfortable asking questions, building practical skills, and progressing at their own pace.

What You Can Expect

  • Friendly and supportive tutors
  • Practical, easy-to-follow learning
  • Small group sessions and personalised support
  • Courses suitable for beginners and improvers
  • A welcoming community environment

Available Courses

Digital Skills

Gain the confidence to use computers, smartphones, email, and online services in everyday life. This course is designed to help you build practical digital skills for work, communication, and accessing important services online. Whether you are completely new to technology or looking to improve your existing knowledge, you will learn in a supportive and friendly environment at your own pace.

CV Writing

Develop the skills and confidence needed to move towards employment, training, or further education. Learn how to create a strong CV, prepare for interviews, improve workplace communication, and understand what employers are looking for. This course offers practical support and guidance to help you take positive steps towards your career goals.

Online Safety

Learn how to stay safe and confident when using the internet, social media, email, and online services. This course covers important topics such as creating secure passwords, spotting scams and phishing emails, protecting personal information, and understanding online privacy. Designed for all experience levels, the course provides practical guidance to help you use technology safely in everyday life, whether for work, communication, shopping, or banking online.

Financial Management

Build practical money management skills to help you feel more confident and in control of your finances. Learn about budgeting, saving, online banking, managing bills, and making informed financial decisions. The course focuses on real-life skills that can support everyday financial wellbeing and help reduce financial stress.

Job Search Skills

Learn how to search and apply for jobs effectively using online tools and modern recruitment platforms. This course will help you improve applications, tailor your CV, prepare for interviews, and understand how to present your skills confidently to employers. Ideal for anyone returning to work, changing careers, or starting their employment journey.

Creative Skills

Explore your creativity through engaging courses in photography, graphic design, digital media, and other creative subjects. Develop practical creative skills while building confidence, self-expression, and problem-solving abilities. Whether for personal enjoyment or future career opportunities, these courses provide a welcoming space to learn and create.
